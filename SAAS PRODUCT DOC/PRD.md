# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

## Product Name: **Cyber Sploi**

---

# 1. Product Overview

Cyber Sploi is an **AI-powered cybersecurity SaaS platform** designed to help individuals, startups, and enterprises detect, analyze, and defend against cyber threats automatically.

The platform combines:

AI penetration testing
AI vulnerability scanning
AI malware analysis
AI blue team defense
Threat intelligence learning system
Automated security reporting
Cybersecurity research feed (Pen Hub)

Cyber Sploi provides **free pentesting access** while advanced analysis, reports, and defense automation are available through paid tiers.

The platform evolves continuously by analyzing global cybersecurity intelligence and improving detection and defense models.
